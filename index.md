Privacy Policy for Deep Anime Recommendations

Effective date: 27/11/2026
Last updated: 27/11/2026

Deep Anime Recommendations ("the App," "we," "us") is a mobile application for discovering and tracking anime. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.
1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App is designed to work without an account and without sending your personal data to us. We do not operate a server that stores your watch history, ratings, or profile information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device using an on-device database (Room) and local preferences:

    Watch history and ratings: The anime titles you add, the status you assign (Plan, Watching, Completed, Dropped), your 1–5 star ratings, and your episode progress.

    App preferences: Your selected mood filters and profile display preferences.

This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly back up your device through your operating system's built-in backup service. We do not have access to it.
3. Third-Party Service: AniList

The App uses the public AniList GraphQL API to search for anime, retrieve anime metadata (titles, episode counts, cover images, genres, scores), and fetch details for recommendations.

When you search for an anime or when the App loads recommendation data, your device sends a query to AniList's servers (graphql.anilist.co). This request may include the search term you typed or the AniList ID of a show being displayed. AniList may receive your IP address as part of the standard HTTP request. We do not control AniList's data practices. You can review AniList's terms and privacy information at https://docs.anilist.co/guide/terms-of-use.

The App does not use AniList OAuth and does not access your AniList account.
4. No Analytics, No Advertising

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising SDKs. We do not track how you use the App, and we do not display ads.
5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the App does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect it from children.
6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your watch history and preferences remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove individual entries from your list within the App (Profile tab, entry menu, "Remove from list"). Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your device. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is no separate deletion request to send us.
7. Security

Your data is stored within the App's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Network requests to AniList are made over HTTPS (TLS). No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the App is designed to minimize risk by keeping your data local.
8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
